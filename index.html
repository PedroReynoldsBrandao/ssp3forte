Do you recognise these signs?

BPH symptoms should not be ignored

Benign Prostatic Hyperplasia (BPH) affects most men over 50. The sooner you act, the better the results.

🌙
Disrupted nights
Waking 2, 3 or more times to urinate, unable to get back to sleep easily.
💧
Weak stream
Reduced urinary flow, post-void dribbling, difficulty starting to urinate.
⚡
Urinary urgency
Sudden, hard-to-control urge to urinate, even when the bladder is barely full.
💔
Sexual health affected
Weak and/or painful ejaculation; poor sexual performance. Erectile dysfunction frequently associated with BPH — treatable with a natural approach.
😓
Chronic fatigue
Poor sleep, fragmented nights and constant discomfort drain your energy and wellbeing.
🔄
Bladder never empty
Persistent feeling of incomplete emptying after urinating.

Synergistic formula

Ingredients with proven efficacy

SSP3-Forte combines the best known plant extracts for prostate health in an optimised formulation.

01
Saw Palmetto (Serenoa repens)
Berry extract traditionally used to support prostate health and urinary symptoms associated with BPH.[1,2]
02
Pygeum africanum
Pygeum africanum bark extract associated with improvement of BPH urinary symptoms, including reduction of nocturnal urinary frequency.[3,4]
03
Beta-Sitosterol
Phytosterol associated with improvement of urinary flow and reduction of post-void residual volume in men with BPH.[5-7]
04
Zinc
Essential mineral for prostatic physiology. Changes in zinc homeostasis have been associated with prostate disease.[8,9]
05
Urtica dioica (Nettle)
Nettle root extract extensively studied in BPH, including combined formulations with Saw Palmetto for urinary symptom support.[10-12]
06
Pumpkin Seed (Cucurbita pepo)
Rich in phytosterols, zinc and essential fatty acids. Clinical studies suggest benefits in improving urinary symptoms associated with BPH.[13,14]
07
Lycopene
Carotenoid antioxidant studied for its potential role in prostate health, with clinical evidence suggesting reduced BPH progression and improved urinary symptoms.[15]
08
Vitamin E
Fat-soluble antioxidant that contributes to the protection of cells against oxidative stress, complementing the action of lycopene and other antioxidants.[16]
